Documentation

A practical field manual for planning a safe, low-impact day hike with the Adventurous Meerkat Trail Supply kit.

  • Choose a Route
  • Pack and Prepare
  • Follow Trail Practices
  • Finish the Hike

Choose a Route

Match the trail distance, elevation gain, and surface to the least-experienced person in your group. Review an official park map and check current closures before leaving.

  1. Estimate the time needed for the full route.
  2. Confirm park hours and daylight.
  3. Share your route and return time with someone.

Pack and Prepare

Carry water, food, a first-aid kit, a charged phone, navigation, and layers for the forecast. Add any medication or personal supplies your group needs.

Use footwear suited to the terrain. Keep frequently used items accessible and protect electronics from rain.

Follow Trail Practices

Stay on marked paths, give other trail users room, and observe wildlife from a distance. Follow posted rules for pets, fires, and campsites.

Pack out all litter, including food scraps. Do not pick plants or disturb rocks and natural features.

Finish the Hike

Let your contact know you have returned. Check yourself and your gear for ticks, clean muddy footwear, and refill supplies before the next trip.
